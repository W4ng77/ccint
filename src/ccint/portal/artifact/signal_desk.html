Most-discussed CVEs

CVE ids are pulled from post text with a fixed pattern, so this table does not depend on any model. It covers all collected cyber posts, not only the Canada-relevant ones. CVSS is NVD's theoretical severity; KEV means CISA has seen the flaw exploited. The two are shown side by side and never merged into one score.

Canadian ransomware victims × discussion

Victims come from ransomware.live leak-site tracking. A post counts as matched when it names the victim organisation or its domain between 14 days before and 30 days after the listing. Common names (for example a national airline) can match unrelated posts; open the posts before citing a match.

Forums and feeds being crawled

Every source must state why automated access is allowed, and the crawler checks robots.txt before each run. A source that robots.txt refuses is skipped and logged, never fetched.
