List every entity in this post that points to CANADA.

**Hard rule.** If the post literally contains the word *Canada*, *Canadian*,
*Canadien(ne)*, or a `.ca` / `.gc.ca` domain, that is a Canadian entity and
you must list it. This holds even when the post is mostly about something
else, and even when the word appears in a hashtag, a flag emoji caption,
a URL, or a dateline such as `📍 Canada`.

Also list:

- A Canadian province, territory or city: Ontario, Québec, Alberta, British
  Columbia, Nova Scotia, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Toronto, Montréal, Ottawa,
  Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Winnipeg, Halifax, Kelowna, Québec City ...
- A Canadian government body, agency, police force, regulator, Crown
  corporation, school board, university, hospital, bank, telecom or company:
  RCMP, GRC, CSE, CST, Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, Centre canadien
  pour la cybersécurité, CRA / Canada Revenue Agency, Service Canada,
  Sûreté du Québec, Commission de la construction du Québec, Elections
  Canada, University of Alberta, Loblaws, Sobeys, Rogers, Telus, Bell,
  RBC, TD, Desjardins, Air Canada, WestJet ...
- A named person in a Canadian public role: a federal or provincial
  minister, a Canadian police spokesperson, a privacy commissioner.

Examples of correct answers:

- `Play Ransomware Targets Inglewood Golf in Canada` →
  `[{"type":"place","text":"Canada"},{"type":"org","text":"Inglewood Golf"}]`
- `🔴 Commission de la construction du Quebec found by Qilin 📍 Canada` →
  `[{"type":"gov","text":"Commission de la construction du Quebec"},
  {"type":"place","text":"Canada"}]`
- `Integriti is hiring a Security Specialist 🌎 Toronto, Ontario, Canada` →
  `[{"type":"place","text":"Toronto, Ontario, Canada"}]`

Do **not** count these, which are the confusions that matter:

- **Canonical** — a Linux vendor, not Canada.
- **GTA** — normally Grand Theft Auto; count it only if the post is plainly
  about the Greater Toronto Area.
- **CRA** in an EU or compliance context — there it is the Cyber Resilience
  Act, not the Canada Revenue Agency.
- **Vancouver, WA** and **Ontario, California** — US places.
- The French language by itself, or France, Belgium, Switzerland.
