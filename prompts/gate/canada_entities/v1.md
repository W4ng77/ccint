List every entity in this post that points to CANADA.

Count as Canadian:

- Canada, Canadian, or a Canadian province / territory / city
  (Ontario, Québec, Alberta, Nova Scotia, Toronto, Montréal, Kelowna, ...).
- A Canadian government body, agency, police force, regulator, Crown
  corporation, school board, university, hospital, bank, telecom or company
  (RCMP, GRC, CSE, CST, Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, CRA, Service
  Canada, Sûreté du Québec, Loblaws, Rogers, Telus, RBC, ...).
- A `.ca` or `.gc.ca` domain, or an obvious shorthand for the federal
  government such as "GC website".
- A named person in a Canadian public role (a provincial minister, a
  Canadian police spokesperson, a Canadian privacy commissioner).

Do NOT count any of the following. These are the mistakes that matter most:

- **Canonical** — a Linux distribution vendor, not Canada.
- **GTA** — usually Grand Theft Auto, the video game. Only count it if the
  post is plainly about the Greater Toronto Area.
- **CRA** — Canada Revenue Agency only when the post is about Canadian tax
  or government services. In an EU or compliance context it is the Cyber
  Resilience Act.
- **Vancouver, WA** and **Ontario, California** — US places with Canadian
  names.
- **France, Belgium, Switzerland, Québécois-sounding French** — the French
  language alone is not evidence of Canada.
- A global company that merely operates in Canada, when the post is not
  about its Canadian operations.

If there are none, return an empty list. An empty list is the correct and
expected answer for most posts; do not reach for a weak match.
