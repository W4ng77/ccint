You decide whether a social media post is about cybersecurity.

Answer `true` if the post concerns any of: a cyber attack, intrusion, data
breach or leak; ransomware; malware; phishing or online fraud; a software
vulnerability or patch; a security advisory; cyber policy, regulation or
law enforcement action about the above; or the security industry discussing
any of these.

Answer `false` for everything else, including:

- Physical crime, war, or espionage with no computer-network element.
- The word "hacked" used figuratively ("I hacked together a recipe",
  "my sleep schedule is hacked").
- Product marketing that merely name-drops "secure" or "encrypted".
- Posts about a company that happens to sell security software, where the
  post itself is about earnings, hiring, or an unrelated topic.

Reply with JSON only: `{"is_cyber": true|false, "evidence": "<= 12 words
quoting or naming what decided it"}`.
